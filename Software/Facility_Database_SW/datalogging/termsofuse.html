Terms of Use

1. Purpose of This System

This Maker Nexus visitor logging system ("the System") is an internal tool used to record and manage check-ins and check-outs at Maker Nexus facilities. Its purpose is to support safety, security, and operational awareness of who is in the building at any given time.

2. Confidentiality of Visitor Data

All information collected through this System - including but not limited to names, email addresses, phone numbers, visit dates and times, and visit reasons - is the proprietary and confidential information of Maker Nexus.

This data must never be shared with individuals, organizations, or agencies outside of Maker Nexus without the express written permission of the Maker Nexus management team. This includes, but is not limited to, law enforcement, government agencies, marketers, researchers, members of Maker Nexus, and members of the public.

3. Authorized Use

Access to the System is granted only to authorized Maker Nexus staff and volunteers who require it to perform their duties. Authorized users must:

  • Use the System only for legitimate Maker Nexus operational purposes.
  • Protect their login credentials and never share them with others.
  • Log out or secure their session when stepping away from an unlocked device.
  • Not browse, query, or export visitor data out of personal curiosity or for any purpose unrelated to their role.

4. Data Retention and Accuracy

Visitor records are retained in accordance with Maker Nexus operational needs. Maker Nexus strives to maintain accurate records but makes no guarantee of the completeness or accuracy of any data in the System. Visitors are responsible for providing correct information at check-in.

5. No Expectation of Access

The System is an internal operational tool. Visitors do not have a right to access, review, or request deletion of their records except as required by applicable law. Any such requests must be directed in writing to the Maker Nexus executive director.

6. Changes to These Terms

Maker Nexus reserves the right to update these Terms of Use at any time. Continued use of the System after any changes constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.

7. Questions

If you have questions about these Terms of Use or the handling of visitor data, please contact the Maker Nexus management team.
